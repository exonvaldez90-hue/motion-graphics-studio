# Reference interpretation

Source: one vertical explainer Reel supplied by the user as a screen recording (1080x1756, 30 fps, 94.4 s, English voice-over). Topic: a spacecraft returning from orbit and being caught by its launch tower. No frames from it are stored here on purpose: the profile must work without the video, and its pictures are not to be reused.

The recording includes the platform's interface (like, comment and share icons, an account row). Those are not part of the style.

## How it was analysed

- Stills every 0.5 s (189 frames), 16 full-resolution stills, 18 frame strips at 10 frames per second around every change of place.
- Per-frame tracking of the red line's position and of overall picture change (2,831 frames).
- Sampled colours, measured line widths and box positions.
- Speech transcription (296 words) and level analysis of the audio.

## Principles versus specifics

Kept as style (in `STYLE.md`): the single sheet, the one thread, paper and ink palette, two type roles, the fixed word-by-word caption box, three image treatments, hold-then-travel motion, hard-cut type, running counters, labelled evidence plates.

Kept as method (in `STORY.md` and `ADAPTATION.md`): result-first loop structure, scale through familiar comparison, known-versus-new turn, mechanism told as constraints, the asked-aloud "why", choosing a visual form from what the sentence does, motivating every camera move.

Left behind as topic-specific: every object and diagram in the table above. The table describes them generically so the function of each station is clear without inviting a copy.

## Limits of this analysis

- The source is a compressed screen recording, cropped vertically, with interface overlays. Colours, sizes and vertical positions are approximate.
- Fonts were matched by eye to open-licence families; the originals were not identified.
- The audio could be measured (pace, pauses, levels) but not listened to. A continuous low bed is present; its musical character and the individual sound effects are unknown. Audio guidance beyond the measurements is a recommendation.
- Easing curves were judged from frame strips, not extracted.
- One video is a small sample. Some things recorded as rules may be choices made for that one topic; the "flexes per video" list in `STYLE.md` marks where that is most likely.
