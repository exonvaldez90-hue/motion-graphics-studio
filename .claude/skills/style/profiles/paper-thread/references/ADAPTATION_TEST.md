# Adaptation test

A dry run of `ADAPTATION.md` on a topic with nothing in common with the reference, done to find gaps in the instructions. It is a record of the test, **not a template**: do not reuse its stations for a real video on this or any topic. All figures below are placeholders marked `[source needed]`; a real production replaces them with sourced numbers or removes them.

Test topic: what happens in the two seconds after you tap a payment card. Language: Czech voice-over. Target: 60 s.

## Run

**Claim.** A tap that feels instant is a round trip through four organisations, and it usually finishes before you lift your hand.

**Step 1, route.** "The red line is the payment message, and it goes from the card to the bank and back to the terminal." Category: process flow. Passes the one-sentence test.

**Step 2, sheet.** A long horizontal sheet. Terminal at far left, issuing bank at far right, two intermediaries between. The thread runs left to right along the top half and returns along the bottom half, so the camera goes out and comes back and the last station is the first one. Scale changes: push in to the chip (smaller than a fingernail), pull out to a map when the message crosses a border.

**Step 5, vocabulary.** Heroes: the card, the terminal (halftone cutouts of real ones). Generic: banks and processors as labelled ink blocks, a matchbox, a paper slip. None of these appears in the reference.

**Step 6, moves.** Every move follows the thread or changes scale with the sentence. Station 9 to 10 had no reason to move, so 10 was placed inside the same view.

**Step 7, density.** Station 8 first had map, three labels, two counters. Cut to one counter and two labels.

## Gaps found and fixed in the profile

1. **Topics with no physical hero or no live footage.** The first draft assumed a photographable subject and a live opening shot. Added to `STYLE.md`: what may serve as a hero (any real artefact, including a document or a device), what may serve as a plate (screenshots, documents, archive scans), and how to open on paper when no footage exists.
2. **The journey axis is not always distance.** Added "time" explicitly to the ruler rule and to Step 1.
3. **Longer words in other languages.** Czech caption groups overflow 16 characters. Added a language note: fewer words per group before smaller type.
4. **Horizontal sheets.** The first draft implied the thread is always vertical. Reworded: vertical is the resting default, the route decides.
5. **Brand overlay.** Nothing said how a studio logo or brand colour fits. Added a short rule to `STYLE.md`.
6. **Counters spanning stations** needed an explicit HUD-or-world rule. Added.

## Verdict

The instructions produced a hook, a beat structure, a thread meaning, fifteen station concepts and motivated camera moves for an unrelated topic without consulting the reference video, and none of the pictures repeat the reference's. What the test cannot show is whether a render looks right; that needs a Remotion preview.
